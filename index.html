The spine

Four instruments come due inside seven days and not one will publish the criterion it turns on.

2 June. Executive Order 14409, 91 FR 34565–34567, starts a sixty-day clock expiring 1 August on a new legal object, the "covered frontier model." The Director of the NSA makes the designation; the benchmarking that sets the threshold is classified in the order's own words; §3(c) disclaims any "licensing, preclearance, or permitting." It attaches to models, not companies. 8–24 July. Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, the Digital Omnibus on AI, adopted on the 8th and published in the Official Journal on the 24th, is in force today as the third day after publication. It defers the AI Act's Annex III standalone high-risk regime to 2 December 2027 and the Annex I embedded regime to 2 August 2028 — but not Article 50 transparency or the penalties, which bite on 2 August at €15m or 3% of worldwide turnover, whichever is higher.

16–23 July. Kimi K3 announced, API only, weights promised for the 27th. WAICO founded in Shanghai on the 16th, twenty-nine signatories, no Western states. On the 22nd the OSTP director accuses Moonshot of covert distillation of Anthropic's Fable, without evidence. On the 23rd the APEC Digital and AI Ministerial adopts the Chengdu Statement, and China publishes a standard system issuing digital identities to AI agents. 26 July. Zhuang Rongwen, Minister of the Cyberspace Administration of China, opens the APEC High-Level Forum on AI in Chengdu; at 22:15 UTC the Wall Street Journal reports Nvidia in talks to provide a roughly $250bn financial backstop for OpenAI's datacenter financing, and nobody confirms it. Today, the EU regulation enters into force and K3's weights are on no reachable surface at 12:35.

The four share one property, and it is not secrecy — Brussels published every word. It is that in each case the operative criterion sits out of the reader's reach by a different mechanism. Washington classified it. Brussels deferred it by annex, so the same statute now runs at two speeds chosen by product category rather than by risk. Beijing put it in domestic standards and exported the vocabulary. Moonshot has not written it yet. The question a governed party would want to ask — does this apply to me — has no addressee in any of the four.

Labour & workforce ·shortFour datasets, four denominators, four answers

The strongest case against today's reading

The log's self-failure rate is not evidence of rigour. It is evidence that the method has not converged — and a method that has not converged cannot produce a dataset.

Take the numbers seriously. The second pass found eleven errors the first missed; the third found eight in the second's twenty entries. There is no principled reason to think the third pass is better than the second, so the honest expectation is that a fourth finds a comparable number in today's. That is not yet a time series of the observatory's error rate — it is substantially a record of one instrument's noise, with the observatory's rate buried inside it.

And the errors are not randomly directed. Today's largest was a false negative: the log instructed, in a standing protocol, that a real, primary-sourced, forty-billion-dollar contract was almost certainly fabricated, because a search of the wrong CIK in the wrong month came back empty. Followed, that instruction would have suppressed a true and reportable fact invisibly. A protocol producing confident do-not-publish findings is strictly more dangerous than one producing confident publish findings, and this one has now produced its first.

The decision it forces, and it is not the control arm. Freeze the taxonomy — twelve codes in three days is a diary, not a taxonomy, and each new code makes the series less comparable rather than more. Then fix the single thing that would have caught the S-1: no claim enters the log without the URL and the date it was fetched. That error survived because the entry recorded a conclusion instead of a search. Do that and the log becomes auditable; skip it and the control arm supplies a denominator for a numerator that is partly noise. And if that is more than the four days before Saturday allow, the right move is the one §8 has pointed at for three days: keep the log as private QA, drop the publication ambition, and write the op-ed. The machinery has produced twelve failure codes, four protocol revisions, and no words that would appear under your name.
